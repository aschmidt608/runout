How it works: your photo(s) go from your device straight to Anthropic, where Claude reads the artist, title, label, catalog number and any visible tracklist. That's then matched against Discogs (if you've connected it — here or on the main Runout page, same login) or MusicBrainz (free, no login), which supplies the real tracklist, cover art, and — when the database has them — direct YouTube videos and streaming links. Links marked solid are direct matches from that data; outlined ones are searches on that service, which almost always land on the right thing for mainstream releases but can miss for obscure or vinyl-only pressings. If neither database finds the record, the tracklist falls back to what Claude read or knows, flagged as such. Photos aren't stored; recent scans keep only a small thumbnail in this browser's local storage.
